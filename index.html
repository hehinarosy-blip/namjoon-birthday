Dear Namjoon,

Today, I wanted to create something that could travel farther than a message and feel warmer than a simple birthday wish.

So I made you a little universe.

A place filled with stars, words, memories and wishes from someone who admires you deeply — and from ARMYs across Africa who continue to carry your music in their hearts.

Thank you for reminding us that growing, changing, questioning and becoming ourselves are beautiful things.

Thank you for your words. Thank you for your music. Thank you for the person you continue to become.

I hope this new year of your life gives you quiet mornings, beautiful skies, meaningful art, good books, laughter and all the peace your heart deserves.

With love from Africa,
Rosy & ARMY

ONE LAST THING

✦

To Namjoon,
and to BTS.

If this little universe could speak, I think it would say thank you.

Thank you for giving us songs that found us when we needed them.

Thank you for giving us words that made difficult days feel a little lighter.

And thank you for reminding ARMY that it is okay to grow slowly, to make mistakes, to change our minds and to keep searching for who we are.

From Africa, we may be far away from Seoul, but distance has never been strong enough to stop music from reaching us.

So on your birthday, I hope you know that there are people under completely different skies who are smiling because you exist.

I hope you and the members continue to find happiness in ordinary days.

Eat well. Rest well. Laugh a lot. Make art. Take beautiful photographs. Look at the sky.

And please keep becoming the person you want to be.

Happy birthday, Kim Namjoon.

From my little universe
to yours. 💜

Rosy & ARMY across Africa
